GoPack Food Delivery App

A mobile-first food delivery app and brand, rebuilt from my very first UI website with a new concept, as a self project to practise wireframing and full prototyping.

Role
UI/UX Designer (self project)
Timeline
2025
Type
Mobile app, self project
Tools
Figma, components, auto layout, prototyping

Origin

Recreated from my very first UI website, with a completely new concept.

Brand

GoPack identity in red, to stand out from other food apps.

Prototype

Scrolling screens, pop-ups and other interactions, built with components and auto layout.

01

Why I rebuilt it

GoPack started from the first UI website I ever designed. I recreated it as a self project with a new concept, to understand wireframing properly and to build a fully working app prototype.

02

Brand identity

I created the GoPack name and brand identity, and made it red because that colour sets it apart from other food apps.

03

From wireframes to screens

I worked from wireframes to high-fidelity screens, so the structure was settled before any visual styling.

04

Components and auto layout

I used components and auto layout throughout, which keeps the design clean and makes every screen consistent.

05

Interactive prototype

The prototype is fully functional: screens scroll, pop-ups open, and the other interactions behave like the real app.
