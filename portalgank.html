PORTALGANK Website

A website concept shaped by research into an institution's manual workflow, designed for the KMIPN VI national competition.

Role
UI/UX Designer
Timeline
Jun 2024 – Jul 2024
Type
Competition project, team
Tools
Figma

Research

The institution's existing manual workflow, studied to find user problems.

Design

Detailed user interfaces, website banners and vector assets in Figma.

Result

The concept placed the team in the top 15 of the competition.
